💑 €5,000 - €8,000👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 €8,000 - €13,000

13-day counter-clockwise self-drive ring road trip through Iceland's most iconic landscapes.

Iceland is the only destination on this list where the land itself still feels unfinished — active volcanoes reshaping the coastline, geysers erupting on schedule, glaciers you can walk on and northern lights overhead in winter. 10-day family fly-in self-drive trip through the Faroe Islands, focused on dramatic landscapes, hikes, villages, sea cliffs, waterfalls and one or two optional island/boat activities.
